RESPONSIVE DESIGN
Mobile-first responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop. Includes a mobile navigation menu, accessible form labels, responsive grids and reduced-motion support.

BEFORE LAUNCH
1. Open script.js and replace BUSINESS_EMAIL = 'hello@yourdomain.com' with the official inbox.
2. Add the official WhatsApp number, social media URLs and any final business contact details.
3. Replace illustrative portfolio concepts with approved client work and verified case studies.
4. Add your final logo and optimised portfolio imagery if available.
5. Test all pages and forms on real devices before publishing.

The contact form uses mailto and does not submit to a backend or CRM. Google Fonts require an internet connection.

To preview locally, open index.html in a browser. For deployment, upload the complete folder to your static hosting provider.
